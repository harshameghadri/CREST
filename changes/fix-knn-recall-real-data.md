- Fixed: the approximate neighbour search (above 20,000 cells, or `exact=False`) lost up to 4% of the true neighbours on average on real embeddings, with up to 8% of cells below recall 0.8, because each cell was searched against the lists around the centre of its own list instead of the lists nearest to the cell itself. Cells now probe the lists nearest to themselves; recall against exact search is at least 0.998 on the eight data sets measured (28,871 to 9.7 million cells), with at most 0.1% of cells below 0.8.
- Changed: `nprobe`, the number of lists each cell probes, is `max(16, ceil(0.5 * sqrt(n_lists)))`. `pp.neighbors(..., nprobe=...)` and `crest.crest.knn_graph(..., nprobe=...)` set it for a higher recall or a faster search, and the value used is recorded in `uns["neighbors"]["params"]`. Neighbour sets above 20,000 cells change compared with earlier releases.
